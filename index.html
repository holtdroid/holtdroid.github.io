In a session

Everything the app does, next to its screen

Each device opens in a tab with its screen, its controls and the tools you'd otherwise wire up over a cable.

The screen, in the tab

Streamed with scrcpy and decoded by WebCodecs: H.264 by default, H.265 or AV1 where the browser decodes them, up to 1080p, with the device's audio and a USB keyboard that types in any layout.

DevTools for every WebView

Debugging is switched on at runtime, so the APK is installed exactly as shipped. Elements, console and network for every WebView in the app.

Recordings

One link instead of a bug report

The server records the session while it runs: the screen, every tap, the WebViews' network, console and WebSockets, logcat, DNS, the VPN exit and the device's load, on one timeline. Drop markers where it went wrong, then share a link.

  • Console and exceptions
  • WebSocket messages
  • DNS and connections
  • VPN location and network conditions

For developers

Your localhost, on the phone

Run the holtdroid client on your computer. Devices you launch reach the ports you publish ashttp://localhost:<port>, and each device's ADB gets a port on your machine, so adband chrome://inspect just work. It goes over SSH through the portal's gateway, with no SSH keys to manage.

Self-hosted

Runs on your cluster, your way

One Go binary and a Helm chart. Sign-in, storage, secrets and VPN are your choice; nothing is tied to a vendor.

node · worker-1readyreadybootingfree

Android in containers

redroid on the node's own kernel, several devices per node, with ARM translation for 64-bit ARM builds.

Real phones too

USB phones on a small host join the same portal, with or without root.

A fair line

Slots per node and per provider. When every device is busy, a launch waits in line; idle devices are reclaimed.

Sign-in your way

Anonymous animal names, a shared password, OIDC, a forward-auth proxy, or users with passkeys and invite links.

anonymouspasswordoidcheaderusers

Observable

Prometheus metrics, OpenTelemetry traces and audit events to stdout, OTLP or a webhook.

No lock-in

Secrets from env, files or OpenBao. Storage on disk or S3. Any VPN that sing-box speaks.
